How it works

Each team's goals are projected separately for 5-on-5 (expected goals + actual goals for/against per 60) and the power play (PP and PK efficiency × penalties drawn and taken), plus other situations. The opposing starting goalie's goals saved above expected scales goals allowed, back-to-backs cut the tired team's scoring ~8% (opponent +6.5%), and team skating speed nudges fast teams up (+2% per step) and their opponents down (−1.7%). Data from the 2020-21 season onward only (MoneyPuck, NHL API, DailyFaceoff, Action Network). Runs on GitHub Actions at 12:01 AM, every hour from 11:17 AM to 10:17 PM ET, and 15 minutes before each game.
