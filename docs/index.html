How it works

Each team's goals are projected separately for 5-on-5 (expected goals + actual goals for/against per 60) and the power play (PP and PK efficiency × penalties drawn and taken), plus other situations. The opposing starting goalie's goals saved above expected scales goals allowed, back-to-backs cut the tired team's scoring ~8% (opponent +6.5%), and team skating speed nudges fast teams up (+2% per step) and their opponents down (−1.7%). From about March, a team already eliminated from the playoffs allows ~4% more goals (and scores 1% fewer). Data from the 2020-21 season onward only (MoneyPuck, NHL API, DailyFaceoff, Action Network). Runs on GitHub Actions: MoneyPuck stats once a day (8:00–11:10 AM ET, as soon as MoneyPuck posts last night’s games), with a model run right after new data comes in; odds, goalies, lineups and projections at 12:01 AM, every hour from 11:17 AM to 10:17 PM ET, and 15 minutes before each game.

Model's chance of 7+ total goals, from the projected total and how evenly matched the teams are (close games get more overtime, shootout and empty-net goals): wins an over 6.5 and an over 6 (exactly 6 pushes an over 6). NHL average ≈ 45%.
