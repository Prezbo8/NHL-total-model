How to read this
  • Total = the nine stats combined, each weighted by how well it predicted a team's future goals in 2021-26 (half a season vs the other half, 320 team-halves). Offense: Goals 21%, xG 21%, SOG 13%, PPG 12%, Att 12%, HD xG 10%, HD 8%, Reb G 3%, Reb 1%. Defense: Goals 26%, xG 19%, SOG 14%, Att 12%, HD xG 9%, HD 7%, PPG 7%, Reb G 4%, Reb 2%.
  • Scoring = goals, power-play goals and rebound goals; Shot quality = expected goals (all, and from high-danger shots) and high-danger shots; Shot volume = shots on goal, shot attempts and rebound shots.
  • Stats are per game and built like the model's ratings: last season pulled a third of the way to league average and worth 15 games, then this season's games. Early in the season they are mostly last season.
  • Model = the goals the model projects against a league-average opponent (what the picks use). Sched = how tough the opponents faced have been, judged by their Total Defense (for offense) or Total Offense (for defense) scores. Adj # = the Total rank after correcting every stat for that schedule.
  • Colors = rank fifths, green = strong, red = weak. The #OFF / #DEF tags on the dashboard use these Total ranks.
  • Click a team to see its last 10 games this season: projected vs actual goals, the total vs the line, and the model's last pre-game call. † = backfilled day (opening line), not in the paper record.

How to read this

vs the model = Net, Scoring, Allowing: how the team did against the model's own projection. luck check = Finish, Goalie: goals vs expected goals, the part that usually doesn't last. schedule faced = how tough its opponents were.

What the columns mean

  • Net: Scoring + Allowing: goals a game better (+) or worse (−) than the model expected, both ends combined.
  • Scoring: Goals scored a game minus what the model projected before each game. + = scoring more than expected.
  • Allowing: Goals the model projected against minus goals actually allowed, a game. + = allowing fewer than expected.
  • Finish: Goals scored minus expected goals (xG), a game, vs the league average. + = finishing chances better than usual (skill or hot shooting).
  • Goalie: Expected goals against minus goals allowed, a game, vs the league average. + = goalies stopping more than expected.
  • Defenses faced: How good the defenses this team played in this window were. Tough = they allow 3%+ fewer goals than an average defense, Easy = 3%+ more, else Average. Tap a cell for how many games were against a top-10 Total Defense or one of the 5 worst, and each opponent with its rank. Scoring already accounts for this (each game's projection includes the opponent); Finish doesn't.
  • Offenses faced: How good the offenses this team played in this window were. Tough = they score 3%+ more goals than an average offense, Easy = 3%+ fewer. Tap a cell for how many games were against a top-10 Total Offense or one of the 5 worst, and each opponent with its rank. Allowing already accounts for this; Goalie doesn't.
  • ▲ / ▼ (Last 30 / 14 / 7): under each number, how much better (▲) or worse (▼) it is than the team's season number; under # (under the team on phones), how many spots the team moved up (▲) or down (▼) from its season rank. = means no change. A window marked '= season' has the same games as the season so far.
  • Every number is per game; + (green) = good for that team, − (red) = bad. Click a column to sort by it.

How to read it

  • Context only, not in the projection. Tested on 2021-26 (research/studies/recent_form.py): a team scoring above or below the model does not keep doing it in the next games (mostly luck), and xG-based form carries over only ~7%, so the model keeps using season-long ratings.
  • Finish and Goalie say why. A team far above expectations mostly because of Goalie (or Finish) is riding hot goaltending (or shooting), which usually cools off.
  • Small windows are noisy. Over 7 days a team plays about 3 games, so Last 7 is mostly noise; Last 30 and the season mean more.
